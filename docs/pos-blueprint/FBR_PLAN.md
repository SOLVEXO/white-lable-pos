## 1. Who must integrate — and do we need a licence?
**Tier-1 retailers must integrate every outlet/POS** for real-time reporting (S1: STA 1990 s.3(9A); S3 Q1). *Tier-1 retailer* (S1, s.2(43A), current text): (a) a unit of a national or international chain; (b) in an air-conditioned mall/plaza/centre (kiosks excluded); (c) cumulative electricity bill over Rs 1.2 million in the preceding 12 months; (d) wholesaler-cum-retailer; **(f) a retailer that has acquired a POS for accepting payment through debit/credit cards from banks or SBP-authorised digital-payment providers**; (g) a retailer whose deductible withholding tax under ITO ss.236G/236H exceeded **the threshold notified by the Board** in the preceding 12 months; (h) any other class the Board prescribes. (The older 1,000-sq-ft limb (e) is struck out in S1; S3 still lists it.) The threshold value and any 2026 change are **not in the documents I could read** (a secondary source quotes Rs 100,000 under SRO 1842(I)/2023 — **to be confirmed**).
* **Restaurants, cafés, coffee shops, eateries, snack bars, hotels** must integrate whether or not they are Tier-1 (S2: Sales Tax Rules Ch. XIV-A, r.150ZA; S3 last Q).
* **Generally:** Sales Tax Rules Ch. XIV (S1, r.150Q) applies to "registered persons or class of registered persons notified by the Board in the Gazette" — i.e. the circle can widen by notification. **2026 draft:** an FBR draft (**SRO 288(I)/2026**, 18-02-2026) would replace Income Tax Rules Ch. VIIA ("online integration of businesses") and reportedly extends mandatory POS/e-invoicing to more business classes **[secondary — KPMG alert says "not yet effective"; the official text must be obtained]**. Income Tax side already in force (S1): ITO s.237A(3) — an "integrated enterprise" may make **no sale without a fiscal invoice**; s.182 penalties; s.191 prosecution.
* **Consequences of not integrating** (S1): s.33 Table 25A penalties Rs 0.5 m → 1 m → 2 m → 3 m with sealing; **s.8B(6): adjustable input tax cut by 60%** (S3's 15% is the older figure); gas/electricity disconnection (s.14AB); ITO s.21(r) expense disallowance up to 8%. **Offence (s.33 No.24):** issuing an invoice without the prescribed invoice number/QR code, with a duplicate number or a counterfeit QR code — Rs 0.5 m or 200% of the tax, up to 2 years; **a software vendor that provides for skimming is liable to the same penal provisions** (S3).
* **Does a POS software provider have to be registered/licensed?** S1 r.150XE: *no person may carry out integration of notified persons through software unless licensed*, **except PRAL** (r.150XF: PRAL is a licensed integrator and provides integration **free of cost** and a free downloadable POS software on the FBR site). r.150XE(3): every POS software generating invoices for the notified person must be integrated **through the licensed integrator**. Licence conditions (r.150XH): company profile, tech staff, ERP/payment-system capacity, PSHA/ICAP registration, 3 years' audited accounts, incorporation certificate, NTN, **paid-up capital ≥ Rs 10 million**, CNICs of directors, no-blacklist undertaking; licence valid **5 years**, non-transferable, no sub-contractors (r.150XJ); fee capped by General Order (r.150XO). Also **invoices must carry "the unique electronic invoicing or POS software registration number"** (r.150R(13)(c)) and each outlet must display a signboard "Integrated with FBR" with that registration number (r.150R(11)). **Implication:** Solvexo has two routes — (A) register the software and post invoices **to PRAL's service** (the S4 route: POS registration via the eFBR portal; PRAL is the free licensed integrator) or (B) become a licensee. *Which route applies to a cloud POS vendor, and whether a software accreditation step exists, is **not stated in S1–S4** (S2 r.150ZB(3)(j) mentions "accreditation of POS system") → document needed (§10).*

## 2. Registration, credentials, environments
From S4 §2–§5 (FBR/PRAL; this is the *Digital Invoicing* spec, written for supply-chain operators incl. retailers — **whether Tier-1 retail POS uses exactly this API is to be confirmed**, see §10):
* **Per business:** log in at `e.fbr.gov.pk` → *Registration → POS Client Registration*: business info (NTN and business name system-generated; STRN; manufacturer yes/no; **POS type Cloud-based / Client-server**; in-house development yes/no; …), contact info (person, mobile, email, address; General/Technical).
* **Per branch (= our store location):** branch name, city, weekly off days, sector, address, contact, **latitude/longitude (mandatory)**, version, franchise yes/no, store timings.
* **Per POS terminal (= our register/device):** branch, **POS identification number (unique)**, POS type **Primary/Secondary**, **MAC address**, **IP address**.
* On success FBR issues a **POS registration number**, referenced when compiling each invoice, and **"a unique ID and Security Token"** (production token shown on the portal). The payload carries **`bposid`**.
* **Sandbox:** the portal button *Generate Test POS IDs*; sandbox endpoints `https://esp.fbr.gov.pk:8244/DigitalInvoicing/v1/PostInvoiceData_v1`, `…/GetInvoiceDetails`, and a variant with SSL certificate on `gw.fbr.gov.pk`. **Production:** `https://gw.fbr.gov.pk/pdi/v1/api/DigitalInvoicing/PostInvoiceData_v1`. Auth: `Authorization: Bearer <token>`.
* **Cloud servers must be whitelisted:** send NTN, hosting company, **public IP**, hosting country to the FBR helpline (S4 §5) → we need **static egress IPs** for the transmitter.
* **Restaurants (S2 r.150ZB(2)):** register every branch and every POS with: POS registration number, business name, branch name/address, POS identification number, registration date. **Provincial** registration is separate (§7).
* **What the owner/each store must do:** hold an FBR-registered NTN/STRN account (iris/eFBR login), register the business, each branch and each POS terminal, obtain tokens, place the signboard, and **notify FBR/the Commissioner within 24 h of any operational failure** (S1 r.150XA(c)(d)).

## 3. Invoice data FBR requires
**Legal minimum particulars (S1 r.150R(13))** — unique FBR invoice no. `XXXXXX-DDMMYYHHMMSS-0001`; verifiable **QR code (7×7 mm)**; software registration no.; **FBR digital-invoicing logo**; seller name, address, registration no.; recipient name, address, registration no.; date; tax period; description, quantity; value excl. tax; sales-tax rate and amount; tax withheld at source; extra tax; further tax; FED in ST mode; total discount; **invoice reference no.**; **HS code**; **unit of measure**; SRO and serial no. — *(s),(t),(u),(z) need not apply to a retailer selling to the general public other than a manufacturer-/importer-cum-retailer (proviso).* Exempt supplies also need e-invoices (r.150R(9)). The POS must: store invoice data, **sign digitally**, transmit securely and receive the FBR number, preserve data irrevocably, generate/print the QR, **close the day/week/month**, and **log every adjustment, modification, cancellation and system event** (r.150R(4)); raise alerts for malpractice (r.150R(6)). Records kept **6 years**, re-creatable at audit (rr.150S, 150T).
**API model (S4 §3.1, v1.2):** master — `invoiceType` (1 Purchase, 2 Sale, 3 Debit note, 4 Credit note), `invoiceDate`, `ntN_CNIC` (buyer/seller; NTN 7 digits, CNIC 13), `buyerSellerName`, `destinationAddress`, `saleType` (8-char code e.g. `T1000017` Goods, `T1000018` Services, `T1000075` standard-rate goods, `T1000080` zero-rated, `T1000081` exempt — Annex 10.1), `totalSalesTaxApplicable`, `totalRetailPrice`, `totalSTWithheldAtSource`, `totalExtraTax`, `totalFEDPayable`, `totalWithheldIncomeTax`, `totalCVT`, `totalDiscount`, distributor NTN/name; **items** — `hsCode` (8 char), `productCode`, `productDescription`, `rate`, `uoM` (code, Annex 10.3), `quantity`, `valueSalesExcludingST`, `salesTaxApplicable`, `retailPrice`, `stWithheldAtSource`, `extraTax`, `furtherTax`, `sroScheduleNo` (Annex 10.2), `fedPayable`, `cvt`, `whiT_1/2`, `totalValues`, `discount`. All money is "numeric up to **two** decimal places".
**Response:** `statusCode`, `errorMessage`, **`result` = the unique sequential invoice number**, `timestamp`, `errors`. The number (13–30 chars) **must be printed with the QR** (S4 §3).
**QR / logo printing:** S1 says **7×7 mm**; S4 says QR **version 2.0 (25×25), 0.70–1.0 inch** — **the two official documents disagree → confirm the current rule** (§10). Logo image is supplied by FBR (S4 shows "use following image"; the file itself I do not have). **Payment mode:** *not a field in the S4 v1.2 model*; the KPRA API has `payment_mode` (1 cash, 2 card) (S5). Whether FBR needs it is **unknown → §10**.
**Buyer:** retail to the general public — name/address/registration of recipient are required by r.150R(13) but S4 marks buyer NTN/CNIC as mandatory on the master record; **how an anonymous walk-in is represented is not stated → §10.**

## 4. Returns, cancellations, credit/debit notes
* **Rule:** debit and credit notes are **issued electronically through the integrated system** and kept 6 years (S1 r.150S(2)). The POS software must handle returns and exchanges and the adjustment must show in the sales reported to FBR; failing that, the retailer records debit/credit notes in **Annex-I** of the monthly return (S3). **Entries in Annex-C cannot be deleted**; a return or cancellation is corrected by **debit/credit notes in Annex-I** (S3).
* **API:** `invoiceType` 3 = debit note, 4 = credit note on the same endpoint (S4 §1.1, §3.1). *How the credit note references the original invoice is **not in the v1.2 master field list I extracted** (r.150R(13)(w) requires an "invoice reference no.") → exact field needed (§10).*
* **Provincial precedent (S5, KPRA):** a separate **credit-note API** — the original is never changed; the credit note has its **own unique number per branch**, `invoice_no` of the original, `note_type` full/partial, reason, and **its own amounts** (not copied from the original); resending the same number returns the saved one (idempotent).
* **Our design consequence:** returns/voids = new **credit-note documents** with own sequence, linked to the original invoice and lines; **never edit** an invoice (G2.5).

## 5. Offline / connectivity
* **Rule (S1 r.150XC):** invoices generated during a failure of the software, internet or power **"shall be clearly identified as invoices issued in the offline mode and shall be uploaded within 24 hours of restoration."** Failures/damage must be reported to the Board and Commissioner within 24 h (r.150XA). A Commissioner may extend integration deadlines up to 60 days, **during which paper invoices continue** (r.150V).
* **No supply may be made except through integrated POS** (r.150R(3); ITO s.237A(3)).
* **Provincial precedent (S5):** retry on 500/network error after 5–10 s; **never retry after 201**; duplicates must be prevented by the POS; a local middleware may store-and-forward.
* **What the paper receipt must show offline:** only "offline mode" identification is specified; **S1–S4 do not say what the receipt shows in place of the FBR number/QR** → §10. Proposal in §8.

## 6. Provincial requirements (separate section — services / restaurants)
Services (incl. restaurants) are taxed by the provinces (SRB Sindh, PRA Punjab, KPRA Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, BRA Balochistan). FBR also has its own restaurant chapter (S2 r.150ZA–150ZE; S3). **Official provincial documents obtained:** **KPRA only** (S5): register the software at `posregistration.kpra.gov.pk`, get POS ID + key per restaurant branch; POST each invoice (`ntn, pos_id, key, invoice_no (unique per pos_id, ≤50 chars), amount, tax_rate, tax_amount, total_amount, date_time, payment_mode`) to `https://kpra.gov.pk/api/rims-integration` over HTTPS → `201` with `transaction_id`; **QR is built by the POS from `pos_id + invoice_no`** (QR URL parameters are in S5's "QR Code API" section); a Windows middleware can store-and-forward. **PRA, SRB, BRA: no official specification obtained** (vendor pages only, **[secondary]**, unverified — including a claim that two provinces cannot run on one branch). Whether provincial integration applies to our target stores depends on **what they sell and where** (a café in Lahore → PRA; Karachi → SRB; Peshawar → KPRA; Quetta → BRA) → **owner decision + documents** (§10). Federal retail goods → FBR.

## 7. Architecture proposal
**Where:** a **server-side transmitter** (new `fiscal` module in `solvexo-api`), not on-device. Reasons: S4 explicitly supports **cloud-based POS** (whitelisted server IP); tokens/credentials never reach tills; one retry engine; offline tills already sync to the server. The app only prints what the server returns.
* **Adapters** like `payments-core`: `FiscalProvider` interface (`submitInvoice`, `submitCreditNote`, `getStatus`) with `fbr_pral` first, `kpra`, later `pra`/`srb`/`bra`; a **mock** adapter for tests and sandbox. Per-store enablement (`fiscalProvider`, environment sandbox|production, `bposid`/POS ids per register, token) — **credentials encrypted with the existing versioned AES-256-GCM key facility (G2.2)**, never logged, owner-gated to edit.
* **Outbox:** append-only `fiscal_documents` (+ `fiscal_events`) keyed by `(storeId, documentType, documentNumber)` — **idempotent**: one transmission row per invoice/credit note; states `pending → transmitting → accepted | rejected | failed_retry | offline_pending`; stores the exact payload hash, response, **FBR invoice number**, QR payload, attempts, timestamps. Worker uses the existing **Mongo `JobLeaseService`** (multi-instance safe) with backoff (5–10 s first retries like S5, then exponential), never retries after `accepted`, dead-letters after N attempts with an alert to *Needs attention*. Duplicate-protection = our unique document number.
* **A sale never waits for FBR.** The sale commits and prints; transmission is asynchronous (target: seconds). If FBR is unreachable the document is `offline_pending`, the receipt is marked **OFFLINE MODE**, and the worker uploads within the **24 h** rule, alerting a manager when approaching it. *Open point:* a printed receipt cannot be updated afterwards, so the printed copy would carry no FBR number — the **digital receipt page** shows the number/QR once accepted. Whether FBR accepts that is a **decision/document (§10)**.
* **Minor units:** we keep integer minor units; FBR wants 2-decimal numbers → convert at the edge; **enable only for PKR stores** (exponent 2); a unit test asserts `minor/100` round-trips and that line sums equal the invoice total (FBR totals are sums of item fields).
* **Audit:** every transmission, retry, rejection and manual retry is an audit event (who/when/payload hash); daily/weekly/monthly **closing** records (r.150R(4)(f)) are produced from the same ledger.
* **Mapping to G2.5 documents:** `Invoice` (immutable, gap-free per-store number) → `invoiceType 2`; `CreditNote` (own sequence, links to invoice + lines) → `invoiceType 4`; line snapshot carries HS code, UoM code, tax category/rate, sale-type code, SRO ref; header carries seller NTN/STRN/name/address and (optional) buyer name/NTN; `fiscal` fields (`externalInvoiceNumber`, `qrPayload`, `transmissionStatus`, `attempts`, `lastError`) are **placeholders on both documents from G2.5**.

## 9. Sizing and risks (estimates, to be re-done once the documents arrive)
* **Fiscal module** (adapter + outbox + worker + audit + tests + sandbox run): ~3–4 weeks engineering; **registration, whitelisting and sandbox access** are calendar-time dependencies on FBR/PRAL.
* **Receipt/invoice printing** (logo, QR, number, offline marking): ~1 week; thermal printer QR size needs a real-device test.
* **Risks:** (1) route A vs B (licence; Rs 10 m capital rule) is unresolved; (2) S4 is the **Digital Invoicing** spec, dated Feb 2024 — the POS-specific/current spec may differ; (3) QR-size conflict (S1 vs S4); (4) provincial APIs for PRA/SRB/BRA unseen; (5) offline receipts without a number; (6) a vendor is liable for features that allow sale suppression (S3) — **no "delete invoice", no editing, every cancellation is a credit note**; (7) the 2026 draft may change scope and rules; (8) **static egress IPs** and secret handling for production tokens.
